(a)
    Pros of Agile Development:
        -allows flexibility to adapt to changes
        -allows faster delivery of working software
        -allows for continuous improvement and feedback from customers
    Cons of Agile Development:
        -less upfront predictability of scope, cost and time
        -can be challenging to scale
        -can cause buggy code to be delivered to customers

(b)
    Scrum Master: Handles all scrum related tasks, which include
    JIRA tooling as well as facilitating sprints and meetings.
    Project Owner: Sets direction for the team, handling releases
    and managing stakeholders.

(c)
    Sprint Planning Meeting:
        -decide what needs to be completed in the coming sprint
        -move stories from backlog to current sprint
        -determine story points and assign stories to team
    Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:
        -10 minutes each day of sprint
        -go over previous day's work completed
        -go over that day's work plan and blockers
    Sprint Retrospective:
        -done at the end of a sprint
        -discuss work completed over the sprint
        -move incomplete work to backlog

(d)
    Epic: Captures a large body of work and is broken down into stories
    Story: Software system requirement that can be expressed in a few short sentences
    Bug: A flaw or fault in the code that needs to be fixed
    Task issue: A unit of work that can be tracked through a workflow
