(a)
    Pros of Agile Development:
        -allows flexibility to adapt to changes
        -allows faster delivery of working software
        -allows for continuous improvement and feedback from customers
    Cons of Agile Development:
        -less upfront predictability of scope, cost and time
        -can be challenging to scale
        -can cause buggy code to be delivered to customers

(b)
    Scrum Master: Handles all scrum related tasks, which include
    JIRA tooling as well as facilitating sprints and meetings.
    Project Owner: Sets direction for the team, handling releases
    and stakeholder management.
